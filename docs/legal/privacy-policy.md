# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 31 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **REBASH Academy** (“we”, “us”) handles information when you visit [https://rebash.in](https://rebash.in/) (the “Site”).

## Who we are

REBASH Academy is an educational website for Cloud, DevOps, and related engineering topics, operated by Shaik Basha. Contact details are on the [Contact](../contact.md) page.

## Information we collect

### Information you provide

If you email us, open a GitHub issue, or contact us through linked social profiles, we receive whatever you choose to send (for example your name, email address, and message content).

### Information collected automatically

When you use the Site, standard technical data may be processed, such as:

- Pages viewed and approximate time on page
- Browser type, device type, and referring URL
- Approximate location derived from IP address (city/region level, not a precise address)

We do **not** require you to create an account to read tutorials.

## Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

| Purpose | Provider | Notes |
|---------|----------|--------|
| Analytics | Google Analytics (Measurement ID configured on the Site) | Helps us understand which pages are useful |
| Advertising | Google AdSense | May show ads and use cookies for ad delivery and measurement |
| Preferences | Site theme / cookie consent | Remembers light/dark preference and consent choices where applicable |

On first visit you may see a **cookie consent** banner. Analytics cookies are used according to that choice where the Site’s consent controls apply. Advertising scripts may still load as configured for AdSense site operation; see Google’s policies for how ads use data.

You can control cookies in your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect Site features.

## Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure aggregated traffic and content performance. Google may process data as described in the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can learn more about Analytics and opt-out options at [Google Analytics opt-out](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

## Google AdSense

The Site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies is described in [Google’s Advertising Privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads) materials. You can manage ad personalisation via [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

## How we use information

We use information to:

- Operate, secure, and improve the Site
- Understand which learning content is helpful
- Respond to messages you send us
- Comply with legal obligations where applicable

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Sharing of information

We share data with service providers that help run the Site, including:

- Hosting and content delivery (for example GitHub Pages)
- Analytics and advertising partners named above

Those providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

## Data retention

Analytics and advertising retention follows the settings of those providers. Correspondence you send us is kept only as long as needed to respond and maintain normal records.

## International transfers

The Site is hosted and services may operate in multiple countries. By using the Site you understand that information may be processed outside your country of residence, subject to applicable law.

## Children’s privacy

The Site is aimed at adult learners and professionals. It is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, or to object to certain processing. To make a request, use the [Contact](../contact.md) page and describe what you need. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

## External links

Tutorials may link to third-party documentation, cloud consoles, or tools. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Site after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions about privacy: see [Contact](../contact.md).
